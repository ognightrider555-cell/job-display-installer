Job Display Installer

Use Chrome or Edge on a computer

This installer talks to the display over USB, which only Chrome and Edge on Windows, Mac or Linux can do. It won't work on a phone, Safari or Firefox.

1Pick the board

2Install

Plug the display into this computer with a USB cable, then click Install and choose its port.

3Set up

Keep the display plugged in. Leave a field blank to keep what the display already has.
